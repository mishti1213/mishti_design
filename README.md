## ✦ ABOUT ME

Hi! I'm **Mishti**, a multidisciplinary designer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Design in UX & Interaction Design at Manipal University Jaipur.

I enjoy exploring ideas, experimenting with visuals and transforming creative concepts into meaningful experiences. My interests lie at the intersection of user experience, visual communication, product design and interactive media.

From designing digital interfaces and physical products to creating graphics and playful game experiences, I love exploring different forms of design and finding new ways to bring ideas to life.

## ✦ FEATURED PROJECTS

### 01. HERLOOP — Period Health Education App
A menstrual and reproductive health education platform designed for teenagers. Focused on making health education accessible, engaging and stigma-free through interactive learning, visual storytelling and a supportive digital experience.

**Focus:** UX Research · UI/UX Design · User-Centred Design

### 02. REDBUS — Usability Testing
A usability evaluation project exploring the RedBus booking experience. Focused on understanding user behaviour, identifying usability issues and evaluating interface, navigation, functionality and performance.

**Focus:** Usability Testing · UX Evaluation · User Research

### 03. FLOWTIP — 3D Product Design
A stylus redesign project exploring product ergonomics, form development and functionality. Focused on creating a thoughtfully designed physical interaction experience through 3D modelling and product exploration.

**Focus:** Product Design · 3D Modelling · Ergonomics

### 04. STORY WEAVERS — 2D Game Design
A creative storytelling game designed to encourage imagination and interactive play. Includes game concepts, visual design, physical story and action cards, and a custom-designed rulebook.

**Focus:** Game Design · Visual Storytelling · Game Mechanics

### 05. NIMBARKA — Website Redesign
A website redesign project for a natural skincare and haircare brand. Focused on visual consistency, information architecture, user experience and creating a cohesive digital brand experience.

**Focus:** Website Design · Information Architecture · Visual Design
